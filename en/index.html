05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before we begin.

Do we need to replace our existing systems?

Not necessarily. Existing equipment, integration protocols and licensing are reviewed first. We then identify what can be integrated and what needs an upgrade. Compatibility is confirmed during technical assessment.

Does every smart building need AI?

No. Integration, central control and automation are a useful starting point. AI is added when suitable data and a clear, measurable use case are available.

Can the project be delivered in phases?

Yes. Solutions can be prioritized by operational needs and budget, with integration planned from the start to support future expansion.

What information helps us get started?

The building type and location, approximate size or floor count, existing systems and the main problem you want to solve. We then define the assessment and proposal requirements.

What are smart building solutions?

Smart building solutions connect access, surveillance, networks, meeting rooms and building management to support monitoring and control. Integration scope depends on building needs and the compatibility of installed systems.

How do BMS, IBMS and AI differ?

A BMS monitors and controls building services such as HVAC. An IBMS integrates multiple systems into a coordinated management environment. AI analyzes data to support predictions and improvements, and can be added when suitable data and integrations are available.

What determines the solution cost?

Cost depends on building size, control points, existing equipment, integration requirements, licensing, implementation and support scope. A proposal starts with reviewing those requirements; there is no single price that fits every building.
