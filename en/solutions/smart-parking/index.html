SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

How does the lane decide who can enter?

The camera sends the plate reading to the lane controller for a permit check. An approved entry triggers an opening request. Local barrier controls govern closing; the operator reviews exceptions and records the decision.

01

Plate camera

Reads the approaching plate and sends the result to the lane controller.

02

Lane controller

Checks the permit and requests opening when access is approved.

03

Barrier unit

Moves the barrier arm using its local controller.

04

Safety detector

Signals vehicle presence or an obstruction to the barrier.

05

Parking platform

Stores permits, access rules and vehicle records.

06

Operator console

Shows exceptions and records the operator’s decision.

Optional extensions Add occupancy sensors and guidance signs, or ticketing and payment, as the site requires.

BEFORE THE SITE VISIT

Questions from the operations team

How do we test Egyptian plates?

Test an approved sample in one lane in daylight and after dark. Log correct readings, misreads and cases that needed manual review. Set performance targets after the site assessment.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Local operation depends on the equipment and configuration. During the pilot we disconnect the internet, check the available entry rules, then verify event synchronization after reconnection.

Does an entrance lane require payment or occupancy sensors?

Space guidance and payment are separate options. Either can be added when the site’s operating requirements call for it.

What does the site team need for a pilot?

Bring the lane layout, vehicle types, peak hours and rules for residents, visitors and deliveries. Review site safety tests with the facilities team.
