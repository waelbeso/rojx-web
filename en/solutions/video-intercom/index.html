Video Intercom Supply and Installation for Residential Compounds

ROJX designs, supplies and installs video intercom systems for property developers and residential contractors in Egypt. We provide entrance panels, indoor monitors and door-release integration to the project specification, with supply-only and installation-only options.

OPERATING SCENARIO

Entry decisions start at the door

The visitor selects the apartment at the entrance station. The indoor monitor shows the visitor and opens two-way audio. The resident requests door release. The controller applies the configured access rule. The door contact reports whether the door is open or closed. Reception reviews exceptions.

The resident requests release, the controller checks permission, and the door contact confirms the door’s position.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Video intercom components

One entrance with local video calling and permission-based door release.

01

Door station

Starts the call and captures visitor audio and video.

02

Call network

Carries calls over IP or a compatible two-wire gateway.

03

Indoor monitor

Displays the visitor and lets the resident request entry.

04

Access controller

Checks permission and sends the lock command.

05

Electric lock

Releases the door when its control circuit is activated.

06

Door contact

Reports whether the door is open or closed.

07

Exit request device

Signals a request to exit where required.

08

Power supply

Provides each device with the required voltage and current.

09

Reception console

Answers routed calls during staffed hours.

PROJECT SCOPE

Intercom project delivery scope

Design and equipment approval
Review drawings and building, entrance and apartment quantities. Develop the design, select models and submit technical documents for consultant approval within the contracted scope.
Supply, installation and configuration
Supply, install and configure approved equipment, connecting indoor monitors with entrance panels, door-release interfaces and an optional reception station. Supply-only or installation-only contracts are available.
Testing, handover and support
Test calls and door release at each point against the agreed acceptance plan. Provide handover documents, training, warranty and support within the scope and periods specified in the proposal.

BEFORE THE SITE VISIT

Questions from technical and procurement teams

Can we contract for supply only or installation only?

Yes. Supply-only, installation and configuration, or full delivery can be scoped after agreeing equipment, documentation and each party’s responsibilities.

What does a compound video intercom proposal include?

The design determines the scope, which may include entrance panels, indoor monitors, networking, power, lock interfaces and an optional reception station. Equipment, quantities, licences and included work are itemised.

Can the system be delivered in phases?

Yes. Each phase’s units, entrances, network requirements and expansion provisions are defined in the agreed design and schedule.

Can existing cabling be used?

We review cable types, lengths, condition and equipment requirements before confirming reuse or a compatible two-wire gateway.

How are warranty and support terms defined?

Warranty periods, coverage, installation and support responsibilities and response terms are stated in the proposal and contract for the selected equipment and scope.
