OPERATING SCENARIO

Entry decisions start at the door

The visitor selects the apartment at the entrance station. The indoor monitor shows the visitor and opens two-way audio. The resident requests door release. The controller applies the configured access rule. The door contact reports whether the door is open or closed. Reception reviews exceptions.

The resident requests release, the controller checks permission, and the door contact confirms the door’s position.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Video intercom components

One entrance with local video calling and permission-based door release.

01

Door station

Starts the call and captures visitor audio and video.

02

Call network

Carries calls over IP or a compatible two-wire gateway.

03

Indoor monitor

Displays the visitor and lets the resident request entry.

04

Access controller

Checks permission and sends the lock command.

05

Electric lock

Releases the door when its control circuit is activated.

06

Door contact

Reports whether the door is open or closed.

07

Exit request device

Signals a request to exit where required.

08

Power supply

Provides each device with the required voltage and current.

09

Reception console

Answers routed calls during staffed hours.

Eight core functions serve the entrance. The copper card identifies the optional reception console.
PROJECT SCOPE

What the project includes

Entrance equipment
Door station, indoor monitor, controller, compatible lock, exit device and power supply.
Optional extensions
Mobile answering, cloud services or SIP integration, subject to licensing.
Site inputs
Door schedule, existing cabling, reception hours and emergency exit requirements.
SITE PILOT

Pilot one door and one apartment

  • Call quality — Check the camera view, speech clarity and routing from the entrance to the apartment and reception.
  • Local operation — Disconnect the internet and record which calling and door-release functions remain available.
  • Door operation — Test authorized release, door-open alerts and emergency egress with the site team.

Bring the door schedule, wiring details and visitor approval procedure.

BEFORE THE SITE VISIT

Questions from the operations team

Who authorizes the door release?

The resident requests release. The controller checks permission before sending the lock command. The door contact reports whether the door is open or closed.

Can existing cabling be reused?

Review the installed cabling to select an IP system or a compatible two-wire gateway. Mobile answering and cloud services depend on the platform and licensing.

What site information is needed to define the scope?

Door schedule, existing cabling, reception hours and emergency exit requirements.
