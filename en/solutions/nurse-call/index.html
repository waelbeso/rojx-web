Hospital Nurse Call

Ward staff can see where a call came from, what help was requested and which calls are still open.

OPERATING SCENARIO

Track calls across the ward

The patient pulls the cord. The ward receives the call location and priority. The call appears at the nurse station and the corridor indicator identifies the room. If the response is delayed, the designated team receives an alert under the ward’s escalation rule. Staff close the call in the room under the ward procedure. The system records closure.

Acknowledgement at the station records receipt. Staff attendance and in-room reset follow the ward’s procedure.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Nurse call components

One ward connects call points and room resets to the nurse station and event log.

01

Bedside call point

Lets the patient request help from bed.

02

Bathroom pull cord

Identifies a call as coming from the bathroom.

03

Room reset unit

Lets staff close the call from the room.

04

Ward controller

Routes calls by location and priority.

05

Nurse station

Displays open calls and records acknowledgement.

06

Corridor indicator

Shows staff which room is calling.

07

Event log

Records the request, acknowledgement and closure.

08

Backup power

Supports agreed ward equipment during a power outage.

09

Staff handset

Receives alerts through a supported interface.

A staff handset is optional and requires an interface supported by the selected system.
PROJECT SCOPE

What the project includes

Ward equipment
Bedside and bathroom call points, room indicators, nurse station, ward controller and backup power.
Optional extensions
Two-way voice, staff handsets or reporting interfaces, depending on the platform.
Site inputs
Ward layout, bed count, shift responsibilities and clinical acceptance criteria.
SITE PILOT

Pilot one room and a nurse station

  • Simultaneous calls — Raise a bedside call and a bathroom call together. Check that staff can distinguish their priorities.
  • Escalation — Leave a test call unanswered and verify that the alert reaches the designated recipient.
  • Fault response — Simulate a link or power outage. Check fault notification, backup operation and recovery.

Include the nursing lead and bring the ward layout and approved call escalation policy.

BEFORE THE SITE VISIT

Questions from the operations team

How does the ward identify the call location?

The call carries its bedside or bathroom location and priority. The nurse station displays it and the corridor indicator identifies the room.

Does acknowledgement confirm staff attendance?

Acknowledgement records receipt at the station. Attendance and in-room reset follow the ward’s approved procedure.

What site information is needed to define the scope?

Ward layout, bed count, shift responsibilities and clinical acceptance criteria.
