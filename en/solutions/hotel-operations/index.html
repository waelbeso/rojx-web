OPERATING SCENARIO

A coordinated stay from check-in to checkout

The front desk changes the room assignment in the property management system. The encoder writes the new room’s access rights and validity period to the card. How the old card is invalidated depends on whether the lock is online or operates offline. Where integration is available, the TV platform clears the previous session and updates the room status.

Test both cards at both doors for the selected lock type. Where TV integration is supported, verify that the previous session is cleared.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Hotel operations components

Two workflows in one hotel: keycard access and TV over the hotel network.

01

Property management system (PMS)

Records check-in, room changes and checkout.

02

Lock platform

Sets the permitted door and validity period.

03

Card encoder

Writes access rights to the guest card.

04

Guest keycard

Carries the access credential for the stay.

05

Door lock

Reads the card and checks permission to enter.

06

Content source

Supplies licensed channels and media.

07

IPTV platform

Distributes channels and services over the network.

08

Hotel network

Carries TV streams to guest rooms.

09

Guest TV or set-top box

Receives the service and plays the selected channel.

Green marks the room-access workflow. Copper marks the TV workflow over the hotel network.

SITE PILOT

One stay, two rooms

  • Key lifecycle — Issue a key, move the guest, then check out. Verify the expected access rights at both doors.
  • Service continuity — Test which tasks the front desk can complete when the PMS interface is unavailable.
  • TV handover — Verify channel delivery and confirm that the previous guest’s TV session is cleared at checkout.

Bring the PMS version, lock models and a guest-room TV to the interface test.

BEFORE THE SITE VISIT

Questions from the operations team

When is the old key invalidated after a room move?

Revocation depends on the lock design and whether it is online. Test both cards at both doors during the room move and at checkout.

How is guest TV updated at checkout?

A supported interface can clear the previous guest session. Confirm the exposed events, licensing and compatible TVs with the hotel.

What site information is needed to define the scope?

Operator approval, door inventory, TV models, channel licenses and hotel network design.
