OPERATING SCENARIO

A clearer view of building operations

The sensor detects a temperature above the agreed threshold. The controller applies the configured alarm delay and control rules. The supervisor identifies the zone and follows the facilities team’s response procedure. The team uses temperature trends and meter readings to investigate the cause and verify the outcome.

Compare the temperature trend and meter readings before and after the intervention to check its effect.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Building and energy management components

Reference design for one cooling zone and its energy meter.

01

Zone sensor

Measures air temperature in the selected zone.

02

Local controller

Compares temperature with the setpoint and adjusts its output.

03

Valve actuator

Positions the valve on the controller’s command.

04

Cooling coil

Transfers heat from the air to chilled water.

05

Energy meter

Records consumption for the equipment or zone.

06

Meter gateway

Translates meter data when protocols differ.

07

Building management server (BMS)

Stores readings, generates alarms and displays trends.

08

Operator console

Displays events and allows authorized adjustments.

09

Energy dashboard

Compares usage with occupancy and operating hours.

BEFORE THE SITE VISIT

Questions from the operations team

What controls the cooling valve?

The local controller compares zone temperature with the setpoint and commands the valve actuator. Facilities staff review readings and alarms at the operator console.

How do we interpret a change in energy use?

Compare similar periods and account for weather, occupancy and operating hours before attributing a change to an intervention.

What site information is needed to define the scope?

Equipment drawings, approved setpoints, read and write permissions, and supported protocols.
