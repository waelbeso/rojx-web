OPERATING SCENARIO

Guest comfort and room status

The guest selects night mode on the bedside panel. The room controller sets the agreed lighting levels and curtain position. The guest can adjust the temperature or lighting locally to suit their preferences. The guest sees the lights and curtains respond to the selected settings.

Night mode changes the room settings. The guest requests housekeeping separately from the panel.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Guest room control components

Local devices control one room. A gateway shares its status with hotel staff.

01

Guest panel

Lets guests select a room scene, request privacy or ask for housekeeping.

02

Thermostat

Measures room temperature and accepts the guest’s setpoint.

03

Room sensors

Detect door status and room occupancy.

04

Room control unit

Applies scenes, guest adjustments and occupancy rules.

05

Lighting outputs

Relays and dimmers operate the lighting circuits.

06

HVAC interface

Sends commands to compatible HVAC equipment.

07

Curtain actuator

Operates motorized curtains where fitted.

08

Hotel gateway

Shares room status with hotel operations systems.

09

Staff console

Displays service requests to authorized staff.

Curtain motors, central monitoring and PMS integration depend on the project scope.
PROJECT SCOPE

What the project includes

Model room
Room controller, guest panel, thermostat, sensors, lighting outputs and HVAC controls.
Optional extensions
Curtain motors, central monitoring dashboards and PMS integration.
Site inputs
Room type, HVAC control method, circuit schedule and hotel operator standards.
SITE PILOT

Pilot one furnished model room

  • Room scenes — Test arrival and night modes and manual adjustments from the guest’s perspective.
  • Service requests — Confirm the meaning and priority of Do Not Disturb and Make Up Room with housekeeping.
  • Offline control — Disconnect the central server and check which controls remain available in the room.

Bring the room plan, circuit schedule and the hotel operator’s guest room standards.

BEFORE THE SITE VISIT

Questions from the operations team

Does night mode request housekeeping?

Night mode adjusts lights and curtains. The guest separately selects Make Up Room or Do Not Disturb on the room panel.

What should the hotel test during a network outage?

Disconnect the central service in a model room and verify which local controls remain available with the selected equipment.

What site information is needed to define the scope?

Room type, HVAC control method, circuit schedule and hotel operator standards.
